Fatemeh Alvankarian

PhD Student, University of Maryland, College Park

I’m a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at University of Maryland, College Park, where I am advised by Prof. Dinesh Manocha. As a member of GAMMA Lab, my current research focus is developing terrain property-aware navigation algorithms for mobile robots. Broadly, I am interested in investigating how multi-modal sensory data can be used to build a belief about the physical properties of materials in interaction with a robot. I received my MSc and BSc in Electrical Engineering (Controls) from University of Tehran.
